# 0005. Licence

- Status: Proposed (decision pending with the author)
- Date: 2026-10-19

## Context

Goal: learners and individuals use Wheelhouse for free; companies that run it in production pay. The v1 proof of concept was published under MIT, and copies already published stay under MIT.

## Options

| Option | Learners | Company evaluation | Company production |
|---|---|---|---|
| MIT or Apache-2.0 | Free | Free | Free |
| PolyForm Noncommercial 1.0.0 | Free | Not allowed without a licence | Paid |
| Business Source License 1.1 | Free | Free (non-production) | Paid, unless covered by the Additional Use Grant; each version becomes Apache-2.0 after a change date of up to 4 years |

## Proposal

Business Source License 1.1, with an Additional Use Grant for educational and non-commercial production use, and a commercial licence for companies. Call it "source-available", not "open source". Outside contributions need a contributor licence agreement.

## Open

The author confirms the licence before the first push of the rebuilt code.
